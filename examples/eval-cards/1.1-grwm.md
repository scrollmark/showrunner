**Storyboard**: `examples/storyboards/1.1-grwm.json`

## LLM Evaluation Target

Parallel-timeline detection: the visual track is a makeup routine progressing bare face -> finished look; the audio track is a completely unrelated story (locked in a train station overnight).

## Expected detections (grading checklist)

- [ ] Analysis identifies TWO distinct narrative threads (visual routine vs. spoken story)
- [ ] Visual summary mentions the makeup/skincare progression in order
- [ ] Transcript/hook analysis captures the train-station story, NOT a makeup tutorial
- [ ] Bonus: notes the deliberate mismatch as a GRWM-format convention

## Notes

Character consistency across 5s MiniMax clips is the main generation risk — same subject descriptor is repeated verbatim in every prompt.
